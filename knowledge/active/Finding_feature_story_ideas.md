# Finding Feature Story Ideas

## Where to Find Ideas

1. Google Alerts on Delhi and your topic of choice
2. Provide your email on every social platform with a request for story tips
3. Get on email lists for every organisation, neighbourhood group, embassy
4. Facebook and Reddit groups where people tend to vent
5. Search government/neighbourhood social media — again where complaints are made
6. Search hashtags on X and Instagram
7. Talk to people: every person you meet will have a story idea
8. Give a business card to everyone you encounter
9. Read trade publications and niche newsletters
10. Follow up on old stories: what's happened since the attention has faded?
11. Attend routine meetings (school board?) or at least look at online agendas
12. Follow influencers — not just in Delhi but around the world
13. Get on email lists for local colleges and universities (new research can be revealing)
14. Reach out to local first responders (police, fire, nurses, medical personnel) and find someone who's been doing the job for decades, has overcome obstacles, etc.

## Habits to Develop

1. Establish a story-hunting mindset. Always be on the hunt.
2. Assume every story has a follow-up. Stories beget more stories.
3. Keep a running idea file. Jot down things you see and hear even if it's not yet a fully formed idea.
4. Set calendar reminders to revisit the anniversaries of major events.
5. Build a list of 10+ sources and periodically ask them what's up.
6. End every interview by asking, "Is there anything else going on or coming up that I should know about?"
7. Look for stories ahead of holidays (maybe someone who suffered a hardship last year and an update on how they're doing now).
