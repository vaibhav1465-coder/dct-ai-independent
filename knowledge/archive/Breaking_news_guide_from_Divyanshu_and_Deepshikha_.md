# Breaking News Guide
### From Divyanshu and Deepshikha

---

## The Goal

What we're trying to fix is the gap between when we have the story and when our readers see it.

Our goal is to build a rapport with the reader so they reach out to us every time they think of news.

## Core Principle

For breaking news, our prime directive remains unchanged — we are not going to play fastest-finger-first, we are not going to send useless, incomplete, unclear news alerts to our users.

The Indian Express stands for clarity, context and journalism that matters. We are going to ensure every news update we send is not only accurate and as quick as possible, but also carries context and purpose — explaining why it is important.

## For Reporters

Send everything the moment it's confirmable. Don't wait for the full picture. Don't wait for the perfectly turned sentence. A fragment of solid information, forwarded immediately, is more valuable right now than a polished paragraph that arrives five minutes later. The desk will take it from there.

## For the Desk

- Alert out within **60 seconds** of confirmation
- Homepage updated within **90 seconds**

Keep headlines tight when writing for the homepage. A shorter headline isn't a lesser headline; it's a better one for that surface. If you've written a long one you love, keep it for the article page and offer a shorter version for navigation.

---

## Breaking News Response Protocol

As soon as a breaking news story is confirmed by a reporter, the designated breaking news editor sends a framed alert text (signed off by the reporter) on the Realtime Desk group, then moves to send a shortened homepage headline.

**Three things happen simultaneously:**

1. **Breaking News Live Blog Manager** — updates blog headline with alert text and appropriate image. Adds a post/card.

2. **Alert Manager** — sends news flash attached with live blog. Moves to creating a breaking page for the article with the same text.

3. **Homepage Manager** — takes live blog as lead story on site or wherever appropriate. Moves to updates with shortened headline. Replaces live blog with breaking story link once up.

All three roles have a back-up person assigned so nothing is lost to a smoke break.

---

That's it. No new philosophy here — just the Express doing what it has always done, a little faster.
