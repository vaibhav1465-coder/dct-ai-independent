# Seven Ways to Boost Engagement

## 1. Start with a compelling, concrete headline

These cannot be an afterthought.

- Research: most people don't read line by line but scan for headlines, bullet points and highlighted elements. A weak headline can sink an otherwise strong article before the audience even reaches the first sentence.
- Data also reveals that questions in a headline consistently outperform declarative ones on click-through rates.
- A good headline tells the audience why they should read the story, not what's in it. This also ensures they remain engaged, unlike when a headline overpromises.

## 2. Subheads matter almost as much

- They're often the second element a reader scans and can determine whether they continue.
- Intersperse your copy with subheads to keep your audience engaged, especially on lengthy articles. Research: subheads reduce cognitive overload and set expectations, section by section.
- Avoid being cryptic. A good subhead can help with SEO (search engine optimisation) especially for an element not included in the headline. Subheads can promote other angles of a story and attract new readers.

## 3. Strong storytelling is the greatest determinant of audience time spent

- Short paragraphs perform better than lengthy ones.
- Narrative transportation: a theory that describes the emotional engagement of the reader when they feel transported to the scene of a story. They temporarily detach from their surroundings while reading and remember it more vividly afterwards.

## 4. Show, don't just tell — visuals are game-changing

- Visuals allow your audience to "see" the scene rather than just process abstract information. They communicate instantly and evoke emotion directly.
- Look for visuals that show something. Hook the reader by weaving that element into the story so they'll pause to take in the photo. Infographics can provide good context outside of a story.
- Try shooting a short cell phone video explaining why you pursued a story. Include any challenges you faced or memorable anecdotes from your reporting.
- Research: Emotionally compelling images and video increase engagement by 70%. Infographics and data visualisation increase time on a page up to 50%.

## 5. Polls and surveys transform a reader from passive observer to active participant

- They create a two-way conversation in a one-way medium. That empowers your audience.
- Tap into relatability. Your readers will be more engaged once an article becomes personally relevant to them.
- Research: interactive content outperforms static content by as much as 81%.
- Ask questions that inform potential follow-up stories. Phrase each option distinctly — one that impacts the reader personally and not framed as a current events quiz.

## 6. Liquid content = highly shareable, viral content

- Online, sharing and not just viewing is the key currency. Consider embedding a short video (under a minute) into your story.
- Make your story practical and useful: "news you can use."
- Research: Specific emotions increase engagement. Anger, awe and anxiety perform better than sadness.

## 7. Respond to all (civil) comments on your social pages and online

- This consistently makes readers come back. They tend to be surprised there is a human who actually reads their comments.
- Trust-building: you never know when the person posting a question may become a valuable source for a future story.
